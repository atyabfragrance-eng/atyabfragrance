Return & Exchange Policy

Home / Return & Exchange

Our Return & Exchange Policy

At ATYAB FRAGRANCE, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We want you to love every fragrance you receive. Here's everything you need to know about our return and exchange policy.

Exchange Policy

  • Products can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery
  • The product must be unused and in its original sealed packaging
  • Exchange is subject to product availability
  • Customer bears delivery charges for exchange shipment

Return Conditions

  • Returns are accepted only for damaged or defective products
  • Contact us within 48 hours of receiving a damaged product
  • Share photos/video proof of damaged product on WhatsApp
  • We will arrange a free pickup and replacement

Non-Returnable Items

  • Products that have been opened, used, or sprayed
  • Products without original packaging
  • Products damaged due to mishandling by the customer

How to Initiate a Return or Exchange

Contact us on WhatsApp at +923331430297 or email us at atyabfragrance@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return/exchange. Our team will guide you through the process.
